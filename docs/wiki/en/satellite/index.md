# Satellite

This area brings together the information needed to ingest, propagate, analyse and validate orbital objects in Orbit.

## Areas

- [Space formats](../formats/index.md): TLE, OMM, OEM, OPM and SP3 contracts.
- [Orbit design and catalogue](../user-guide/manual-orbits.md): manual orbits, catalogue sources and ephemeris operations.
- [Propagation](../propagation/index.md): propagators, numerical integrators and force models.
- [Analysis](../analysis/index.md): trajectory comparison, plots and statistics.
- [Engineering foundations](../engineering/index.md): states, orbital elements, frames and time scales.
