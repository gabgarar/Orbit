# Two-body: time and frames

[Propagation](../index.md) · [Two-body](../two-body.md) · [Time, EOP, and ITRF](../../operations/time-eop.md) · [Reference frames](../../engineering/reference-frames.md)

## Epoch and temporal advance

Two-body receives a design epoch and normalizes every query to `UTC`. The
propagator calculates \(\Delta t=t-t_0\) in seconds and advances mean anomaly
by that interval. UTC defines the orbit instant and published metadata; it
does not change Keplerian dynamics or by itself convert output to Earth-fixed
coordinates.

## EME2000 is the native frame

The state generated by `TwoBodyPropagator` is Earth-centred and inertial in
`EME2000`. It is not TEME and must not be labelled generically as “ECI”. Ideal
dynamics are entirely calculated in that frame; requesting ITRF is a later
transformation, not a new propagation.

## From EME2000 to ITRF

For a map, station, or renderer, `state_at` requests
`EME2000 → CIRS → TIRS → ITRF` from `FrameTransformService`. This differs from
SGP4's classic `TEME → PEF → ITRF` path: it uses IAU 2006/2000A
celestial-terrestrial reduction when `pyerfa` is available.

The transformation uses several time scales for different reasons:

- UTC identifies the API epoch.
- TT enters the celestial quantities of IAU reduction.
- UT1 sets Earth's rotation angle.
- \(\mathrm{DUT1}=\mathrm{UT1}-\mathrm{UTC}\), polar motion, and other EOP
  corrections complete Earth orientation.

The chain and data policy are described in [Time, EOP, and ITRF](../../operations/time-eop.md).

## Velocity, precision, and provenance

Position is transformed with the frame matrix. Velocity includes the time
derivative of that matrix; in particular, it contains the dominant kinematic
term from Earth rotation \(\omega\times\mathbf r\). The derivatives
are also used when carrying acceleration or covariance. See
[Cartesian states](../../engineering/cartesian-states.md) for details.

ITRF output precision depends on EOP and frame-reduction availability, in
addition to the simplifications of the two-body model. Orbit retains
transformation provenance to distinguish versioned EOP from the visual
UTC≈UT1 approximation.
