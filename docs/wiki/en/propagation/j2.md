# J2 force model

## J2 in Cowell

Cowell evaluates the zonal contribution at each RK4 stage using the polynomial of
Legendre grade 2. The composition may also include J3, J4 and drag. Your
Fidelity is limited by the available force pattern and the fixed pitch of
60s.

For \(q=z/r\), the term applied by Cowell is:

$$
P_2(q)=\frac{3q^2-1}{2},\qquad
T_2=3P_2(q)+qP'_2(q),\qquad
Z_2=3qP_2(q)-(1-q^2)P'_2(q),
$$

$$
\mathbf a_{J_2}=\mu J_2R_\oplus^2
\begin{bmatrix}
xT_2/r^5\\
yT_2/r^5\\
Z_2/r^4
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

## Limits

### Variables, units and Orbit use

\(x\), \(y\), \(z\), \(r\), and \(R_\oplus\) are km; \(q\), \(P_2\), \(T_2\), and \(Z_2\) are dimensionless; \(J_2\) is dimensionless and \(\mu\) is km³/s². The resulting acceleration is km/s² and `cowell-rk4` adds it to central gravity at every RK4 stage when `j2` is in `force_terms`.

- The [analytical J2 propagator](j2-analytical.md) does not lose energy or model drag.
- No route implements full geopotential, tesseral/sectoral terms,
  tides, temporal coefficients or adaptive integration.
- J2 does not change the output contract: manual states are still
  `EME2000` before an explicit transformation.

For a fixed numerical preset compatibility there is `j2-j3-j4`, which does not
accepts drag and should not be presented as a configurable `cowell-rk4`. See
[J3](j3.md) and [J4](j4.md) for the remaining implemented zonal terms.
