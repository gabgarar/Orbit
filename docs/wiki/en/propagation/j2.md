## Two different implementations

Orbit offers J2 in two contexts that should not be confused.

| Route | Entry | Method | Drag |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `j2` Propagator | Manual Keplerian elements | First order analytical secular rates. | Not available. |
| `cowell-rk4` with `j2` | Manual Cartesian state | Zonal acceleration integrated by RK4. | Can be combined with `drag`. |

## J2 Analytical Propagator

The model preserves semimajor axis, eccentricity and inclination and applies rates
secular to RAAN, periapsis argument and mean anomaly. Use the radio
equatorial and internal \(J_2\), and generates the native state `EME2000`.

The velocity vector incorporates the contributions of the RAAN rates and
periapsis argument, so that it is consistent with the preceding geometry
that delivers the model.

## J2 in Cowell

Cowell evaluates the zonal contribution at each RK4 stage using the polynomial of
Legendre grade 2. The composition may also include J3, J4 and drag. Your
Fidelity is limited by the available force pattern and the fixed pitch of
60s.

For \(q=z/r\), the term applied by Cowell is:

$$
P_2(q)=\frac{3q^2-1}{2},\qquad
T_2=3P_2(q)+qP'_2(q),\qquad
Z_2=3qP_2(q)-(1-q^2)P'_2(q),
$$

$$
\mathbf a_{J_2}=\mu J_2R_\oplus^2
\begin{bmatrix}
xT_2/r^5\\
yT_2/r^5\\
Z_2/r^4
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

## Limits

- The analytical propagator does not lose energy or model drag.
- No route implements full geopotential, tesseral/sectoral terms,
  tides, temporal coefficients or adaptive integration.
- J2 does not change the output contract: manual states are still
  `EME2000` before an explicit transformation.

For a fixed numerical preset compatibility there is `j2-j3-j4`, which does not
accepts drag and should not be presented as a configurable `cowell-rk4`. See
[J3](j3.md) and [J4](j4.md) for the remaining implemented zonal terms.
