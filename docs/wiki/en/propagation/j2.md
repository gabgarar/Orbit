## J2 in Cowell

Cowell evaluates the compatibility zonal contribution at each RK4 stage using
the degree-2 Legendre polynomial. Composition can include J3, J4, and other
compatible forces, but not `geopotential`. Fidelity is limited by available
force model and fixed 60 s step.

For \(q=z/r\), the term applied by Cowell is:

$$
P_2(q)=\frac{3q^2-1}{2},\qquad
T_2=3P_2(q)+qP'_2(q),\qquad
Z_2=3qP_2(q)-(1-q^2)P'_2(q),
$$

$$
\mathbf a_{J_2}=\mu J_2R_\oplus^2
\begin{bmatrix}
xT_2/r^5\\
yT_2/r^5\\
Z_2/r^4
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

| Symbol | Meaning | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| \(x,y,z\), \(r\), \(R_\oplus\) | Position components, position norm, and equatorial radius. | km. |
| \(q\), \(P_2\), \(T_2\), \(Z_2\), \(J_2\) | Reduced coordinate, polynomial, and zonal factors. | Dimensionless. |
| \(\mu\) | Earth's gravitational parameter. | km³/s². |
| \(\mathbf a_{J_2}\) | Zonal acceleration applied by Cowell. | km/s². |

`cowell-rk4` evaluates this acceleration at every RK4 stage when `j2` belongs to `force_terms`; the adapter converts it to SI when publishing the state.

## Limits

### Variables, units and Orbit use

\(x\), \(y\), \(z\), \(r\), and \(R_\oplus\) are km; \(q\), \(P_2\), \(T_2\), and \(Z_2\) are dimensionless; \(J_2\) is dimensionless and \(\mu\) is km³/s². The resulting acceleration is km/s² and `cowell-rk4` adds it to central gravity at every RK4 stage when `j2` is in `force_terms`.

- `j2` is a historical route with an approximately fixed terrestrial axis in
  `EME2000`. For tesseral/sectoral harmonics, use `geopotential` with local
  ICGEM and strict ITRF path; the two are mutually exclusive to avoid double
  counting.
- Tides, temporal coefficients, and adaptive integration remain outside the
  current scope.
- J2 does not change the output contract: manual states are still
  `EME2000` before an explicit transformation.

For a fixed numerical preset compatibility there is `j2-j3-j4`, which does not
accepts drag and should not be presented as a configurable `cowell-rk4`. See
[J3](j3.md) and [J4](j4.md) for the remaining implemented zonal terms.
