# J3

[Home](../index.md) · [Propagation](index.md) · [Force models](force-models.md) · [J2](j2.md)

## Availability

J3 is implemented as an optional zonal term of `cowell-rk4` and in the fixed
`j2-j3-j4` preset. It is not part of the analytical J2 propagator.

## Applied equation

For \(q=z/r\), Cowell uses the degree-3 Legendre polynomial:

$$
P_3(q)=\frac{5q^3-3q}{2}.
$$

The zonal acceleration evaluated at every RK4 stage is:

$$
T_3=4P_3(q)+qP'_3(q),\qquad
Z_3=4qP_3(q)-(1-q^2)P'_3(q),
$$

$$
\mathbf a_{J_3}=\mu J_3R_\oplus^3
\begin{bmatrix}
xT_3/r^6\\
yT_3/r^6\\
Z_3/r^5
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

## Limits

### Variables, units and Orbit use

\(x\), \(y\), \(z\), \(r\), and \(R_\oplus\) use km; \(q\), \(P_3\), \(T_3\), \(Z_3\), and \(J_3\) are dimensionless; \(\mu\) is km³/s². Thus \(\mathbf a_{J_3}\) is km/s² and is only added inside `cowell-rk4` or the `j2-j3-j4` preset.

J3 is only available in Cowell's numerical composition. It does not turn
Orbit into a full geopotential model or add tesseral, sectorial, tidal or
versioned-coefficient terms. See [J4](j4.md) and
[full geopotential](full-geopotential.md).
