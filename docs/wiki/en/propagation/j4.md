# J4

[Home](../index.md) · [Propagation](index.md) · [Force models](force-models.md) · [J3](j3.md)

## Availability

J4 is implemented as an optional zonal term of `cowell-rk4` and in the fixed
`j2-j3-j4` preset. It is not part of the analytical J2 propagator.

## Applied equation

For \(q=z/r\), Cowell uses the degree-4 Legendre polynomial:

$$
P_4(q)=\frac{35q^4-30q^2+3}{8}.
$$

The zonal acceleration evaluated at every RK4 stage is:

$$
T_4=5P_4(q)+qP'_4(q),\qquad
Z_4=5qP_4(q)-(1-q^2)P'_4(q),
$$

$$
\mathbf a_{J_4}=\mu J_4R_\oplus^4
\begin{bmatrix}
xT_4/r^7\\
yT_4/r^7\\
Z_4/r^6
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

## Limits

### Variables, units and Orbit use

\(x\), \(y\), \(z\), \(r\), and \(R_\oplus\) use km; \(q\), \(P_4\), \(T_4\), \(Z_4\), and \(J_4\) are dimensionless; \(\mu\) is km³/s². Thus \(\mathbf a_{J_4}\) is km/s² and is only added inside `cowell-rk4` or the `j2-j3-j4` preset.

J4 is only available in Cowell's numerical composition. It does not enable a
configurable gravity-field model. For a higher degree-and-order model, see
[full geopotential](full-geopotential.md).
