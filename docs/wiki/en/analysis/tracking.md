# Tracking

[Analysis](index.md){ .md-button }

Orbit does not provide a tracking chain of actual observations. The
application can render propagated objects, calculate visibility from
stations and maintain a local catalog, but does not receive telemetry streams
nor does it correlate measurements with an orbital solution.

## Status

**Not available:** ingestion of real-time measurements, radar tracking or
optical, GNSS tracking, track association, observation quality,
sensor calibration and operational alerts based on measurements.
